# Product

## Platform

web (Cloudflare Pages Worker)

## Users

Companies, shops, and organizations in Japan, China, and elsewhere that need a website, a web system, a mini program, design work, or ongoing IT support — often across Japanese, Chinese, and English.

## Positioning

Toumyou (東緲合同会社) is an Osaka-based IT company: website production, web application and business-system development, e-commerce and payments, WeChat mini programs and mobile, UI/UX and brand design (including video editing), and IT operations and maintenance with AI-assisted automation. One team handles planning through operation, in three languages.

## Constraints

- Public site is trilingual (ja default, zh, en) with a top-right language switcher.
- Do not use fastener, hardware, or procurement content anywhere on the site.
- Keep existing modules: articles, shop and product pages, cart, Google login, account, Stripe checkout and webhook, inquiries, support desk, media library, admin.
- Do not invent client names, case results, revenue, staff counts, certifications, or delivery-time promises.

## Product principles

- Visitors should understand within seconds what Toumyou builds and how to start a conversation.
- Copy is plain and concrete, written natively for each language rather than literally translated.
- The contact form and the diagnostic order are the main conversion paths.
