# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Toumyou serves founders, business owners, brand leaders, overseas operators, and growth teams who need a serious digital partner for content growth, traffic acquisition, short-video production, website production, software development, and commercial IP operations.

## Product Purpose

The website should make visitors quickly understand that Toumyou is a media operations and digital growth company, not a small freelancer portfolio. Success means qualified clients ask for a growth consultation, content system, website, software tool, or multi-channel media operations plan.

## Positioning

Toumyou combines content strategy, short-video production, multi-channel distribution, website/software execution, and AI-assisted growth operations into one operating system for brands and commercial IPs.

## Operating Context

Visitors evaluate the company from a browser before contacting it. The site must communicate authority, scale, systematic thinking, and the ability to operate across content platforms, search, social, websites, software, and conversion funnels.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Toumyou front-facing pages should remove fastener/anchor/hardware positioning.
- Ximiaokeji remains a separate Chinese fastener business and should not be unintentionally changed.
- Current stack is a Cloudflare Worker in `_worker.js`.
- Existing admin, D1, R2, customer, cart, and Stripe systems remain available.
- Toumyou public navigation should focus on media operations and growth services, while `/shop` remains available as Japanese files / paid digital products with the original test link and payment flow.
- Claims must stay strategic and capability-based; do not invent real client names, revenue numbers, or case results without evidence.

## Brand Commitments

- Brand name: Toumyou / TOUMYOU.
- Desired perception: large, professional, modern, calm, strategic, and execution-capable.
- Professional tags to support: content growth leader, traffic acquisition expert, brand digital marketing operator, commercial IP operator, new-media matrix architect, vertical content director, brand narrative and digital communication strategist, multi-channel content experience designer, AI-driven content growth officer, digital media production and operations architect.

## Evidence on Hand

- Existing website code and digital-service history in `_worker.js`.
- No verified client logos, testimonials, revenue metrics, or named case studies were provided; future work must not fabricate them.

## Product Principles

- Strategy must be visible: show how content, channel, traffic, conversion, and data connect.
- Production must feel operational: not only “creative,” but repeatable, measurable, and shippable.
- Copy should sound like a growth partner speaking to business decision-makers.
- The public site should prioritize consultation and trust over ecommerce behavior.
- Keep recovery paths: use Git commits/tags before major pivots.
